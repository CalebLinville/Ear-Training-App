How to run the car test
  1. Pair your phone with the car over Bluetooth and set the car volume to a comfortable level while parked.
  2. Mount the phone on the dash. Leave this page open; the screen is kept awake while it runs.
  3. Turn on Hands-free loop and Speak feedback, press Start, and keep your hands on the wheel.
  4. Do 15–20 questions each: parked, city driving, highway. Note which conditions fail.
  5. Press Copy results and paste them back to Claude so we can see how the recognizers held up.
